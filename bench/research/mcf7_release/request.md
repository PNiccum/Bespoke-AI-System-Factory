MCF7 cells were deprived of FBS for 48 h and released for 24 h in serum-enriched cell culture medium. The lab has a
list of 95 proteins, a selected subset of the proteins that went up after release. Objective: identify new proteins and
pathways that drive the aberrant proliferation of ER+ breast cancer cells.

The lab also has the full measured table that the 95 came from: spectral counts for about 3,300 proteins in three
control (nonproliferative, serum-starved) and three treatment (proliferative, released) replicates. Use it to check the
95 against the data, to compute the changes yourself, and as the background for enrichment.

Build a pipeline I can run on that list (and on the next list the lab sends) that answers:

1. Is this protein set indicative of release into the cell cycle and proliferation? Which phases, pathways and
   biological processes are represented?
2. Are there proteins that do not support the expected outcome, for example proteins that suggest the cells did not
   enter the cell cycle, are arrested, or are apoptotic?
3. Which proteins are already drug targets (approved or in trials)? Which have been proposed as putative targets in
   the literature? Which are already known, or suggested by other studies, to matter in cancer?
4. From the pathways represented and the current literature, which are the most promising candidates for revealing
   new mechanisms of aberrant cancer proliferation, and what new hypotheses does the dataset support?
5. What follow-up questions, more specific and targeted, should be asked next?

Every statement about a protein must cite where it came from (a UniProt entry, a database record, or a PubMed ID),
so the lab can check it.
